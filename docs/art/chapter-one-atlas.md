# Chapter one sprite atlas

`assets/room/chapter-one-atlas-v1.png`, 1536×1024 RGBA. Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, edit/reference mode, transparent background requested. Reference: `assets/room/stage-5.png`. Copied unchanged from the generated-images output into the project. Alpha analysis confirmed transparent background pixels. No Python or bitmap postprocessing was used.

Nine props: parcel closed/open, photo front/back, cassette, tea, Mikhalych portrait, radio, postcard. `CHAPTER_ART` in `chapter-one-ui.js` defines per-prop viewports; each SVG uses an explicit clip rectangle, so adjacent cells cannot leak into letterboxed space. A detail of the photo also supplies the lit window in the finale. Bounds were read from the alpha components, not used to rewrite the PNG.

## Generation prompt

Create a production game sprite atlas matching the attached cozy workshop's painterly chunky pixel-art style: amber lamp light from upper right, cool blue shadow edges, warm aged russet walnut palette, visible discrete pixel clusters, no smooth vector outlines. Transparent background. Exactly THREE columns by THREE rows of equally sized rectangular cells, each prop entirely within its own cell, centered with generous 15% transparent margins, no crossing cells, no grid lines, no labels or text. Nine cells in reading order: (1) closed small brown cardboard parcel tied with thin string, 3/4 view from above, (2) same parcel OPEN with folded flaps, cream tissue paper and empty interior, same viewpoint, (3) aged cream-bordered rectangular photograph showing a rainy apartment courtyard with one blue-framed lit window and a tiny piano silhouette, no writing, (4) back of same aged photograph, nearly blank cream paper with a faded postmark and small blue corner stamp, no handwriting, (5) black 1990s cassette tape with cream label and two visible reel holes, viewed almost straight from above, (6) small cream teacup and saucer full of amber hot tea, 3/4 view, no steam, (7) kindly elderly repairman neighbour portrait, waist-up, bushy grey eyebrows, short grey moustache, knitted brown cardigan over teal shirt, relaxed closed-mouth smile, facing slightly right; no background or frame, (8) old walnut tabletop radio with three brass knobs and green tuning scale, frontal 3/4 view, (9) small ivory thank-you postcard with a hand-painted blue window, green plant and yellow lamp, no text. All objects hand-painted textured pixel art in the same scale of pixel clusters as the reference. This is an atlas of isolated sprites only, NOT a mockup or a full room scene. No typography, watermarks, drop-shadow rectangles or UI buttons. Preserve genuinely transparent alpha.
