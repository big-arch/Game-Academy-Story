## Финальный промпт

Edit the cabinet design shown in reference 2 into a standalone transparent-background GAME SPRITE, matching the hand-painted chunky pixel art of the workshop in reference 1. Output ONLY the EMPTY wooden wall-mounted cabinet, isolated, wide horizontal aspect approximately 4:1, very small transparent margins. Exactly TWO rows of SEVEN cubbies, equal openings, no objects or text. This is a functional background behind separately rendered collectible devices. Replace the flat beige UI-like rectangle with aged dark reddish-brown walnut boards matching the room wall and left wooden cupboard: irregular pixel grain, knots, chipped edges, thick dark wood front edges, restrained muted russet highlights, deep cool brown recesses. Subtle near-frontal perspective consistent with the room: camera slightly above the cabinet, a narrow top face visible, very shallow right side visible, vertical edges vertical, shelf front edges almost horizontal. Keep the inner 7-column by 2-row front grid regular so thumbnails fit. Gentle cool blue window fill from above, faint warm amber rim ONLY from the right, NOT glowing orange timber. Strong contact shadows under top and middle shelf, no black floating drop shadow, no rounded corners, no smooth gradients or vector-looking thin lines. Match the reference's pixel cluster size and textured painterly treatment. The cabinet must feel like an old piece of furniture built into the wall below the window, not an inventory panel. Do not include any part of the room, floor, window, plants, lamp, cat, labels, red annotations, UI or tools. Preserve genuinely transparent alpha around the outer silhouette.
