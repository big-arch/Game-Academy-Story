# Chapter two — One Last Screening / Последний сеанс

Unlock: deliver Anna's parcel in the first room story. The chapter runs independently of the repair order; no replacement of the customer's device or resetting their repair. English is the default and all chapter copy also exists in Russian.

Anna asks for a courtyard screening of Lev's old home video. The invitation provides two practical clues: backup power is limited to 80 W and the scenes run “folded, launched, waved”. The player assembles an evening for the neighbours rather than fixing another interchangeable device.

1. Read the invitation on the desk.
2. Select television (45 W) and player (25 W). The optional lamp (15 W) overloads the 80 W supply. A wrong configuration explains the mistake and allows retrying without damage or penalty.
3. Arrange three illustrated scenes. Order can be undone one step at a time; montage can be solved before or after the power puzzle.
4. Align focus marks and confirm the image. Changing equipment or the cut invalidates focus, preventing an obsolete successful check from bypassing changed preparation.
5. Choose the closing still: the boat or the neighbours. Both finish the chapter, with different reactions from Anna and a matching physical still on the desk. Award 120 coins once. Notebook and chosen ending persist across reloads and repair orders.

Optional: examine Lev's mark and find his sentence about giving objects another evening. This can also be discovered after the screening; it is not a condition for the reward. Future stories can refer to this saved fact without forcing everyone to find it.
